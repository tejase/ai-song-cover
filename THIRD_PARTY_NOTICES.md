# Claude Animation Base

The easing, deterministic hash and animation-on-twos helpers in
`src/remotion/animation.ts` are adapted from
https://github.com/JohnHeibel/ClaudeAnimationBase (`src/core.js`).
Our scene illustrations and Canvas renderer are original CoverReel code.
No source song, character artwork, or recorded media from PDoomVideo is bundled.
